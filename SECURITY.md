# Security Policy

Report vulnerabilities to **security@gambitapp.io**. Do not open a public issue.

Please include:

- A description of the vulnerability and the affected surface.
- Steps to reproduce or a proof-of-concept.
- Your assessment of the impact.
- Whether you want coordinated disclosure and any credit preferences.

We aim to acknowledge within 3 business days and to have a fix or
mitigation plan within 14 days for high-severity issues.
